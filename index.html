Review cycle timeline

1
Go to Employment Hero and log in
Open your browser and go to app.employmenthero.com. Enter your work email address and password, then click Sign in.

2
Complete two-factor authentication (2FA)
After signing in you'll be asked to verify your identity. Choose whichever option suits you — both are equally valid.

Choose how you'd like to verify your identity each time you log in.

Authenticator app Recommended Use Microsoft Authenticator to generate a 6-digit code

SMS text message Receive a one-time code sent to your mobile number
Option A — Microsoft Authenticator: Open the app on your phone, find the Employment Hero entry, and enter the 6-digit code shown.

Option B — SMS: Select the SMS option and a verification code will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter it when prompted.
3
Click Performance in the left sidebar
Once logged in, look at the left-hand navigation bar. Click Performance — it has a teal circular icon and sits between Development and Benefits & Perks.

The review deadline is 18 June 2026. Your review auto-saves as you go — click Save Draft if you're not ready to submit, or Publish once all questions are answered.

If you have any trouble accessing your review, come and chat to your HR team at Aspira Financial.
